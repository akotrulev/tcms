CI ingestion → CI results. "Ingestion" is the system's verb for what it did; "results" is the reader's noun for what they came to look at.

Why a tab and not a row

▸
The rail is ordered by how often a screen is opened. Schedules and CI results are opened weekly or less; giving each a permanent row pushed Work and Test cases further from the top for every reader, every day.
▸
A tab is where the reader already is. Whoever wants a schedule was looking at runs a moment ago — the two are the same subject at different times.
▸
Nothing is hidden by this. The badge on CI results is visible from the Runs tab, so the reason to open it is on screen before it is opened — the same test a folder had to pass in the first draft.
▸
And the URL is untouched, so the tab is discoverable navigation added to a route that already worked, not a move.
