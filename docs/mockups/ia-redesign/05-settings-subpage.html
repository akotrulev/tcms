Statuses, kinds and the moves that are legal between them

Statuses

11 · three kinds · used by 3 boards
New
NewNot started. The default for a new issue, at any level.
kind: not started
Refining
RefiningBeing groomed. Where an epic or a story earns its readiness signals.
kind: not started
Ready to develop
Ready to developThe hand-off out of grooming. Was To do — the new name says who it is ready for.
kind: not started
In progress
In progressSomebody is on it. Starts the cycle-time clock.
kind: in progress
In review
In reviewWaiting on a reviewer.
kind: in progress
Ready for test
Ready for testThe hand-off to QA. A drop here is the arrival on the QA board.
kind: in progress
In test
In testA tester has it.
kind: in progress
Ready to deploy
Ready to deployTested, not shipped. The tester's last word; the PO's next drag is the deploy.
kind: in progress
Done
DoneShipped. Stops the clock, and is what release readiness reads.
kind: done
Rework
ReworkOff the line — the return path from review or test back to a developer.
kind: in progress
Blocked
BlockedOff the line, and on every board. Remembers the status it came from; counts as unfinished in its epic.
kind: in progress
Reordering has a keyboard twin on every row — WCAG 2.2 Dragging Movements. 3 not started · 7 in progress · 1 done. Changing a status kind changes what "complete" means on every chart in Analytics.

Out of Blocked is the remembered return, not a listed move: a blocked card goes back to the status it came from, which is why it is one status and not nine.
