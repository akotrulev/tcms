Where the boldness is spent: the wide uppercase wordmark and the single magenta stroke. Nothing else in the interface is allowed to be loud.
  • Most distinctive of the three — a magenta-accented QA tool is not a thing anyone else is shipping.
  • Risk: magenta next to failure red on a dense results screen. Mitigated by shape, but it is a real cost and worth a live look before committing.
  • Cost: largest change to index.css — new action colour, new dark-mode pair, two new font families.
