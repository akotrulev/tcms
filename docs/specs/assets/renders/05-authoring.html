4. Amount rules

4.2 A refund request MUST be rejected if its amount exceeds the captured amount of the payment. The rejection carries AMOUNT_EXCEEDS_CAPTURE and writes no ledger entry.

Testedby TC-193, TC-194 · last run passed

4.3 Partial refunds accumulate. The sum of all refunds against one payment may not exceed its capture, and a request that would cross the line is rejected in full rather than truncated.

5. Timing

A refund may be issued while the refund window is open. The window closes 180 days after capture; after that the request is rejected with WINDOW_CLOSED.
