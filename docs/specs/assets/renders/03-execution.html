The verdict is a number key

1 passed, 2 failed, 3 blocked, 4 skipped — printed on the buttons, not hidden in a help page. A tester with a phone in one hand records a step without finding a target.ui-reasoning · Productivity Tool · keyboard-shortcuts is must_have

Steps ahead are dimmed, not hidden

The current step is the only one at full ink and it carries a 2px steel edge. The rest stay legible, because a tester reads ahead to know what is coming.Was: every step at equal weight in a table, with the current one marked by a radio.

"Last time" is on screen before it is needed

What this case did in the previous run, and where it broke, sits in the rail at rest. A tester who hits the same failure knows within a second whether it is news.

The timer is a field, not a stopwatch you must stop

It counts from the moment the case opened and is editable in place. How long it took (seconds) asked a person to remember; this asks them only to correct it.
