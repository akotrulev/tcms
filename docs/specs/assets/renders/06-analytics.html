A plan is a scope decision plus its evidence. Out of scope is prose because the reason is the artifact — a list of excluded ids says nothing a reader can check.

The line chart has a crosshair by default

Not on request. An SVG chart in a browser is interactive; shipping one without a hover layer wastes the medium and forces the reader to estimate against gridlines.interaction · hover layer

One series, so no legend box
