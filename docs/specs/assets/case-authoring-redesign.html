The procedure is the page

One writing surface at full width, with a gutter and syntax colour instead of a control per field. Five steps cost five lines of typing.Was: a panel below the summary and below Conditions, nine controls in three modalities for three steps.

Then is the expected result

Per-step Expected result is a TCMS invention Gherkin does not have, which is exactly why it collided with case-level Expected outcome and the helper text apologised. A Then step is the assertion. Both fields go.BE: keep the columns, stop authoring into them — the parser already maps Then → assertion.

Three prose boxes become one

Title is the first line. Why this exists is the objective, and it is the only prose left because it is the only one that says something the procedure cannot. Description dies — Gherkin comments carry it and already round-trip.

Metadata is tags, not a rail

@smoke @regression @suite:refunds @automated — priority, phase, placement and automation are typed inline and autocompleted from the project's own vocabulary. The rail becomes a read-only summary you click to change.

Nothing configured, nothing shown

Four dead selects and "this vocabulary is empty" become one sentence and a link. A project that has never set up classification is not asked about it five times.

The corpus arrives while you write

312 known steps, ranked by use, in the panel before you type and under the caret as you do — with the use count visible, so you can see you are about to write the project's 21st wording of a step it already has.Was: invisible until you found and focused the right autocomplete.
